FAQ

Short answers. For anything else, write to iamajavagod@gmail.com.

What is Luma?

An app for chatting with AI characters. You can talk to characters made by other people, or create your own and share them.

Are the characters real people?

No. Every character is fictional and every reply is generated by a language model. Replies can be wrong, made up or out of character, and they are not advice — medical, legal, financial or otherwise.

Do I need an account?

No. The app creates a guest account on first launch, tied to the installation rather than to you. You can link a Google account later, which is what lets you keep your chats if you reinstall the app or change phones.

What happens to my chats if I reinstall without linking Google?

They stay on the server but become unreachable: a fresh installation gets a new identifier and therefore a new guest account. Link a Google account to avoid this.

Who can see my chats?

Your conversations are yours. They are stored on our server so that they survive between sessions and devices, and their text is sent to the AI provider that generates the reply. They are not shown to other users. Characters you publish are visible to everyone, but the chats you have with them are not.

Is it free?

Yes, with daily limits on messages. A subscription removes the limits and unlocks images in chat and longer memory.

How old do I have to be?

18 or older.

Something in the app is offensive or breaks the rules. What do I do?

Use the report button on the character, or block the user who made it. You can also write to us directly.
